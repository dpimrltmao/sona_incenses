The Bukhoor ritual

Three simple steps.

Place a small piece of Bukhoor over safely prepared charcoal or an electric burner, let the fragrance bloom, then allow the smoke to move gently through your space.

Always use a heat-safe burner on a stable surface and never leave burning charcoal unattended.

  1. 01WarmPrepare your burner safely.
  2. 02PlaceAdd a small amount of Bukhoor.
  3. 03EnjoyLet the scent settle into the room.
